## 1. Product overview
- **One-liner:** Practical AI implementation for businesses in Georgia — 2–3× less routine work.
- **What it does:** Builds AI assistants (WhatsApp, Telegram, website, voice), automates CRM and document workflows, and trains teams. Every project starts with a free process audit and a fixed-price pilot.
- **Category / how people search:** AI implementation agency, AI chatbot / assistant development, business process automation, AI training for teams (Georgia, Tbilisi, Batumi).
- **Type:** B2B service (implementation + training + optional monthly support).
- **Pricing (public, GEL / USD):** Team training from 1,900 ₾ / $700 · Tool implementation from 3,200 ₾ / $1,200 · Custom development from 6,700 ₾ / $2,500 · Support from 550 ₾ / $200 per month. Free audit; pilot price fixed before work starts.
- **Languages:** Georgian, English, Russian (site and solutions).

## 2. Target audience
- **Companies:** SMB and mid-market in Georgia with many repetitive enquiries or documents. Priority industries: hotels and apartments (Adjara/Batumi, Tbilisi), wholesale and distribution, clinics, restaurants, retail/e-commerce, real estate and developers, logistics, tourism, services, education.
- **Departments:** sales, customer support, finance/accounting, HR, marketing, procurement, back office, leadership.
- **Decision-makers:** owner / CEO, commercial director, operations director, head of sales/support.
- **Jobs to be done:** answer every enquiry fast (24/7, three languages); stop manual data entry into CRM/1C; prepare proposals and documents in minutes; give the team practical AI skills.

## 3. Personas
- **Owner / CEO** — wants growth without hiring; fears wasted money on “AI hype”. Promise: measurable pilot, fixed price.
- **Operations / commercial director** — owns process KPIs (response time, CRM completeness, cost per lead). Promise: one workflow automated in 2–4 weeks with a success metric.
- **Team lead (sales, support, front desk)** — overloaded by repetitive messages. Promise: AI handles routine, people keep decisions.
- **Technical influencer / IT** — worries about integrations and data. Promise: amoCRM, Bitrix24, 1C, Google Workspace, WhatsApp, Telegram; enterprise APIs; on-premise option with GPU servers.

## 4. Problems & pain points
- Enquiries pile up in WhatsApp/Telegram/Instagram; slow replies lose deals and guests.
- Managers retype orders from PDFs and chats into CRM/1C.
- Proposals, reports and document checks eat hours.
- Teams “tried ChatGPT” without process, templates or review rules.
- Fear of losing control: AI making promises, discounts or diagnoses on its own; data leaks.

## 5. Competitive landscape ⚠
- **Direct:** local and Russian-speaking AI/chatbot agencies, freelancers on Upwork/Telegram.
- **Secondary:** chatbot SaaS (e.g. constructor platforms), CRM vendors’ built-in AI.
- **Indirect:** hiring more staff; doing it in-house with ChatGPT.
- ⚠ Named competitors in Georgia not yet researched.

## 6. Differentiation
- Starts from revenue and process, not technology; free audit with an impact estimate and a fixed pilot price.
- Georgian-language AI for local customers; three languages out of the box.
- Control built in: review points, human handoff, role-based access, review log; data never trains public models.
- One accountable lead from audit to launch; direct line via Telegram/WhatsApp.

## 7. Objections & anti-personas
- “How much does it cost?” → public starting prices + fixed pilot.
- “What if AI makes a mistake?” → review points, handoff to people, source citing.
- “Will we have to replace our systems?” → integrates with existing CRM/1C/messengers.
- **Not a fit:** companies wanting a generic chatbot for 50 ₾; projects without any repetitive volume.

## 8. Switching dynamics
- **Push:** missed enquiries, manual CRM entry, overloaded staff.
- **Pull:** faster replies, complete CRM, hours saved, measurable pilot.
- **Habit:** “we reply ourselves”, fear of changing tools.
- **Anxiety:** cost, data safety, AI errors, lock-in.

## 9. Customer language
- Use: «заявки», «рутина», «ответ за секунды», «карточка в CRM», «пилот», «бесплатный аудит», “workflow”, “free AI audit”.
- Avoid: hype (“revolutionary”, “magic”), unverified percentages, “neural network does everything”.

## 10. Brand voice
- Calm, concrete, businesslike; short sentences; numbers over adjectives.
- Personality: practical, transparent, local, accountable.
