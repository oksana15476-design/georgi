# 06-90-day-plan · 2026-10-29 · process

## en

“Where do we even start with AI?”

A plan that works for most businesses:
• Weeks 1–2: audit one workflow and agree one metric
• Weeks 3–6: pilot on real data, with a person approving
• Weeks 7–10: roll out to the team and write the rules
• Weeks 11–13: start the second workflow, reusing what you built
