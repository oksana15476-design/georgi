## en

In most recruitment agencies, the hours do not go on finding candidates. They go on reading CVs that do not fit, agreeing interview times and answering “any news?”.

AI can score each CV against the brief with written reasons, reply to every applicant the same day and offer interview slots from both calendars.

Consultants make every shortlist and every decision. Under UK GDPR, that is also the safe way to design it.
