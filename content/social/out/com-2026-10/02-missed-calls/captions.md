How much does a missed call cost you?

A quick way to check, with your own numbers:
1. Count the calls that went to voicemail last week.
2. Estimate how many were new customers.
3. Multiply by the value of a first order or appointment.

Example with illustrative figures: 15 missed calls a week, one in five a new customer worth £150. That is about £1,900 a month walking to a competitor.
