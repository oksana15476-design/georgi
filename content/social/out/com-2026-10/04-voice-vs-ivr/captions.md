# 04-voice-vs-ivr · 2026-10-22 · myths

## en

“Press 1 for bookings, press 2 for…” Most callers hang up before option 4.

An AI voice agent works differently. The caller says what they need in their own words, and the agent books, answers or passes the call to a person.

What it should never do on its own: give clinical, legal or financial advice, quote prices outside your rules, or keep an upset caller on the line.
