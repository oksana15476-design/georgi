# 07-knowledge-base · 2026-11-03 · tips

## en

A support chatbot is only as good as what it reads.

Before the bot, build the knowledge base:
• start from questions customers actually ask, not from your brochure
• one approved answer per topic, with an owner and a review date
• prices and policies from the live source, not a copy
• a clear rule for what goes to a person

How to build and test an AI knowledge base: https://praxenai.com/blog/ai-knowledge-base-for-support?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=organic_com_2026-10&utm_content=07-knowledge-base
